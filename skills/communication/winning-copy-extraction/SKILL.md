# Winning Copy Extraction

Find the reusable patterns in your best-performing emails.

## What to Look For

### Subject Line Patterns
- Length: short (< 5 words) vs. long?
- Format: question vs. statement vs. name drop?
- Personalization: did using their name/program/situation improve opens?
- Curiosity vs. clarity: which performed better?

### Opening Line Patterns
- Did it start with them or with you?
- Was it specific to their situation or general?
- Did it reference a trigger (application, prior email, cohort date)?

### Body Structure
- How long were the best performers?
- Did they use bullet points or paragraphs?
- Was there a story, a fact, or a direct value statement?

### CTA Patterns
- What was the ask? (Reply, click, schedule, confirm)
- How many CTAs per email?
- What phrasing worked? ("Does this still make sense?" vs. "Schedule a call"?)

### Tone
- Formal or casual?
- Urgent or low-pressure?
- First person or we/team voice?

## Step 1 — Analyze the Copy

From `$ARGUMENTS`, review the provided examples. For each, note the patterns above.

## Step 2 — Extract the Playbook

Find what the best performers have in common. Build a repeatable template from the patterns.
