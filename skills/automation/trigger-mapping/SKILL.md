# Trigger Mapping

Identify which of 12 trigger types apply and what to do about each.

## The 12 Trigger Types

### High-Urgency Triggers (act within 24-48 hours)
1. **Application submitted** — peak interest moment
2. **Reactivation date reached** — they said "contact me then"
3. **Cohort start imminent** (< 7 days) — last chance urgency
4. **Inbound reply received** — they initiated, respond fast

### Medium-Urgency Triggers (act within 3-5 days)
5. **Offer email opened 3+ times** — reading but not replying, something's holding them back
6. **Cohort approaching** (7-30 days) — natural urgency window
7. **Prior sequence completed, no response** — needs a new angle
8. **Price drop occurred** — trigger re-engagement with new offer

### Low-Urgency Triggers (act within 1-2 weeks)
9. **Reactivation date approaching** (within 30 days) — prep the outreach
10. **Similar cohort starting** (different product, same category) — alternative offer
11. **Alumni success story** — social proof relevant to their goal
12. **New product or program launched** — relevant to their interest

## Step 1 — Map the Triggers

From `$ARGUMENTS`, identify which triggers apply to the prospect or audience. For each active trigger:
- Name the trigger
- Assess urgency
- Recommend outreach action and timing
